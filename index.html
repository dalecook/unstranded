How to play

    
    
      
  • Find the theme words hidden in the grid. The clue tells you what they have in common.
    • 
      
  • Drag through letters, or tap them one at a time and tap the last letter again to submit. Letters can connect in any direction, including diagonally.
    • 
      
  • Theme words turn blue. The spangram names the theme and stretches from one side of the board to the other.
    • 
      
  • Every letter is used exactly once.
    • 
      
  • Theme words never cross over themselves or each other. Other words can cross their own path and can reuse letters from words you've already found.
    • 
      
  • Spot a shorter word that fits the theme (like CROW for birds)? That's a stepping stone, and it earns you a free hint. Other words of 4+ letters fill the hint meter.
    • 
      
  • Everyone gets the same puzzle first each day. After that, press New puzzle to play on through this month's collection.
